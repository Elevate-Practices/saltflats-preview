What changes for a patient on a phone.

Each fix targets a moment where a visitor drops off before they book.

Nothing jumps while it loads

Today the row of office cards moves as the page loads, so thumbs land on the wrong button. The new layout reserves space for everything up front.

Call and Book always in reach

A bar pinned to the bottom of the screen keeps Call and Book online one tap away on every scroll.

Easier to read

The faint light-blue labels and footer text are replaced with darker tones that pass contrast checks, so the page reads well in bright Utah sun.

Lighter and faster

Photos are sized for each screen and there is almost no code to download, so the page appears in about a second, even on a phone.

The $99 offer up front

The new patient visit is the first thing visitors read and has its own section, so price-shoppers have a reason to book now.

Pick your office, then book

Three clear office cards with Medicaid status, phone and booking, so families pick the right office the first time.

Current-site scores are from Google PageSpeed Insights runs on October 31, 2026 (phone speed ranged 80 to 85 across runs). New-site scores are from Google Lighthouse lab tests of the new homepage.
