Common questions.

Is professional whitening safe?

Yes. Professional whitening is done with your dentist's guidance to protect your teeth and gums.

How long do veneers last?

With good care, veneers can last many years.

Does insurance cover cosmetic work?

Usually not, but some plans cover bonding that repairs damage. We'll check your benefits.

Can I see what my smile will look like?

Ask your dentist at your consultation about previewing the result.
