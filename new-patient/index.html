Common questions.

What's included in the $99 visit?

A comprehensive exam, X-rays and a basic cleaning. If you need more care, you get a written estimate before anything is scheduled.

Which office should I pick?

The one closest to you: Fashion Place in Murray, Herriman, or South Bangerter in Riverton. Every office offers the $99 visit and online booking.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major dental plans, including Delta Dental, Regence, Select Health, PEHP, EMI Health and DMBA. Call or text your office to check your plan. See all plans.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes, at our South Bangerter office in Riverton (385-533-9393). Our Fashion Place and Herriman offices do not accept Medicaid.

I get nervous at the dentist. What can you do?

Tell us when you book. TVs on the ceiling, noise-cancelling headphones, laughing gas and oral sedation are all on offer.

Can my kids come too?

Yes. We see the whole family, and many families book back-to-back visits so it's one trip.
