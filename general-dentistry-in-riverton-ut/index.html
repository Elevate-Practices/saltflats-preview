Care that keeps small problems small

Regular exams and cleanings catch cavities and gum problems while they're still easy to fix. Salt Flats Dentistry sees patients at offices in Murray, Herriman and Riverton, so there's usually one close to home or work.

What happens at a checkup

  • A cleaning that removes plaque and tartar from the places brushing misses
  • An exam of your teeth, gums and bite
  • Digital X-rays when you need them
  • A clear plan and a written estimate before any further treatment is scheduled

Tooth-colored fillings

When we find a cavity, we repair it with a natural-looking, tooth-colored filling so your smile stays your own.

Common questions.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Most people do best with a cleaning and exam every six months. If you have gum problems, your dentist may suggest coming more often.

How much is a first visit?

New patients start with a $99 visit that includes an exam, X-rays and a basic cleaning, at any of our three offices, with or without insurance.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major dental plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Call or text your office to check your plan.

Which office should I choose?

Any of them. Pick the one closest to you. Medicaid is accepted at our South Bangerter office in Riverton only.
