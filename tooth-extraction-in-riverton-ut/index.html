Same-day crowns

Common questions.

Will I be asleep?

We offer laughing gas and oral sedation to help you relax. Your dentist will talk through the right option for you.

How long does it take to recover?

Most people feel better within a few days. Wisdom teeth can take a little longer.

What can I eat afterward?

Soft, cool foods for the first day or two. Skip straws and smoking while it heals.

Can a missing tooth be replaced later?

Yes. Implants, bridges and dentures are all options.
