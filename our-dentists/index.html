Our dentists

Meet our dentists

Four doctors who treat patients across our Murray, Herriman and Riverton offices.

Owner · Dentist

Dr. Todd Vincent, DDS.

DDS · Creighton University School of Dentistry

Dr. Todd Vincent grew up in Brigham City, Utah, and earned his dental degree from Creighton University School of Dentistry. He served a religious mission in Guatemala.

He's a dentist, a business owner and, in his words, a devoted family man. He and his wife have five children. Off the clock you'll find him camping, boating or in his woodshop.

Dentist

Dr. Dave Powell, DDS.

DDS

Dr. Dave Powell brings 25 years of dental experience to Salt Flats Dentistry.

He's married with three sons, and spends his free time hiking and going to concerts.

Dentist

Dr. Handro Martinez, DMD.

DMD · Kornberg School of Dentistry, Temple University

Dr. Handro Martinez was born in Ecuador and was raised bilingual in New Jersey. He earned a bachelor's degree in community health from Utah Valley University and his dental degree from the Kornberg School of Dentistry at Temple University in 2019.

He has volunteered his dental skills in Ecuador, Mexico and Guatemala. He and his wife, Angelica, have four children. He coaches youth soccer, cycles, rock climbs, snowboards and loves a cold plunge.

Dentist

Dr. Trenton Schroeder, DDS.

DDS · University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry

Dr. Trenton Schroeder studied at Kansas State University and graduated from the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry.

He's an avid golfer in the summer and hits the slopes once the snow falls.
