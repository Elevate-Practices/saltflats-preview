Emergency Dentistry in Herriman: common questions.

Can I get a same day emergency visit near Herriman?

Yes, when the schedule allows. Call 385-766-1717 early. The office is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

What if I knock out a tooth?

Keep the tooth moist if you can, call immediately, and come in as fast as possible. Time matters for the chance of saving it.

Do you handle emergencies in Spanish?

Yes. Dr. Handro Martinez treats patients in Spanish and English at Herriman.
