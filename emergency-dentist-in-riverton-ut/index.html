Emergency dentist in Riverton, UT

Pain, a broken tooth or a lost crown can't wait. Call your office and we'll get you in as soon as we can.

Call first, then come in

Call your nearest office as early in the day as you can and tell us what's going on. Our offices offer same-day emergency appointments when the schedule allows, for new and returning patients.

What counts as a dental emergency

  • A toothache that won't go away or keeps you up at night
  • A cracked, chipped or broken tooth
  • A lost filling or crown
  • Swelling in your gums or face

Fixed fast, sometimes in one visit

Broken tooth? With CEREC, many crowns are designed, milled and placed the same day. Learn more about same-day crowns.

If it's a medical emergency

For trouble breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding or a serious injury to the face or jaw, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Same-day crowns

Common questions.

Can you see me today?

Our offices offer same-day emergency appointments when the schedule allows. Call as early in the day as you can.

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the top, not the root, rinse it gently and try to put it back in place or keep it in milk. Call your office right away.

I'm a new patient. Can I still come in for an emergency?

Yes. New and returning patients can call for an emergency visit.

How much does an emergency visit cost?

It depends on what you need. You'll get an explanation of your options and costs before treatment.
