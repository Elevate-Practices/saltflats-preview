Same-day crowns: what one visit looks like

How a CEREC same-day crown works at Salt Flats Dentistry: a digital scan, a crown designed and milled in the office, and placed before you leave.

Most crowns take two visits and a temporary in between. At Salt Flats Dentistry, CEREC technology lets us make your permanent crown in the office, the same day.

Step 1: a digital scan.

A digital scanner takes a 3D picture of your tooth. It replaces the goopy impression trays most people remember.

Step 2: design and mill.

Your crown is designed on screen, then milled right in the office while you relax.

Step 3: done before you leave.

Your new crown is placed the same day. No temporary, and no second trip.

Is it right for my tooth?

Your dentist will look at the tooth at your exam and tell you whether a same-day crown is a good fit. Ask about it when you book.
