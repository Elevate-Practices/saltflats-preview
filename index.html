Questions

Good to know.

How much does a first visit cost?

$99 for a comprehensive exam, X-rays and a basic cleaning, at all three offices, with or without insurance. If you need more care, you get a written estimate before anything is scheduled.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most major dental plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Call or text your office to check your plan.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes, at our South Bangerter office in Riverton (385-533-9393). Our Murray and Herriman offices do not accept Medicaid.

Can you see me today for an emergency?

Each office holds time for broken teeth, lost crowns, swelling and pain. Call as early in the day as you can and we'll work you in when the schedule allows.

I get nervous at the dentist. What can you do?

Tell us when you book. You get extra time, a slower pace, headphones and a TV above the chair, plus laughing gas or oral sedation if you'd like.

Can I book online?

Yes. Pick your office above and choose a time. New and returning patients can both book online.
