Good habits start early

We see kids from their first teeth through their teen years. Short, friendly visits help children get comfortable, and parents leave with simple tips for brushing and snacks at home.

What a kids' visit includes

  • A gentle cleaning and a check of growing teeth and bite
  • X-rays only when they're needed
  • Tips for brushing, flossing and healthy snacks
  • A plan you can understand if anything needs care

Fun to look at, easy to sit through

TVs on the ceiling give kids something better to watch than the light, and noise-cancelling headphones help sensitive kids relax.

CEREC same-day crowns

One visit. One crown.

Most crowns take two visits and a temporary in between. Ours are made in the office while you relax.

  1. 1

    Scan.

    A digital scan replaces goopy impression trays.

  2. 2

    Design and mill.

    Your crown is designed on screen and milled in the office.

  3. 3

    Done.

    It's placed before you leave. No temporary, no second trip.

Common questions.

When should my child first see the dentist?

Around their first birthday or when the first tooth comes in. Early visits are short and help kids feel at ease.

Do you accept Medicaid for kids?

Our South Bangerter office in Riverton accepts Medicaid. Our Murray and Herriman offices do not.

Can I stay with my child?

Yes. Parents are welcome in the room, especially for the first few visits.

How do I book my child?

Pick your office on our locations page and book online, or call the office directly.
